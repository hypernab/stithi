Awareness

WHY MOVEMENT MATTERS

Falls can affect independence and confidence. Regular strength, balance and mobility activities can support healthy ageing and fall prevention.

WHEN STITHI FLAGS A CHANGE

  1. Pause
  2. Check your surroundings
  3. Regain balance
  4. Continue only when comfortable
  5. Seek appropriate assistance when needed
Care & awareness

A SHARED PICTURE

STITHI can help provide a simple picture of movement patterns for discussion with a caregiver or healthcare professional.

Planned next step: a caregiver SMS system for consent-based movement updates and supportive check-ins. It will be designed with privacy, clear opt-in, and appropriate escalation in mind.
